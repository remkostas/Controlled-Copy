# WI-QUA-004 Handling Damaged or Suspect Material at Receipt (Revision 2)

> Synthetic demonstration document. Kestrova Components GmbH is a fictional company. Not for operational use.

## 1 Purpose

This work instruction tells receiving staff what to do when material arrives damaged, wet, unsealed or possibly contaminated. It applies at all sites.

## 2 Outer packaging damaged, product intact

If the outer packaging is damaged but the product inside is intact, accept the delivery. Take photos of the damaged packaging, post the goods receipt to blocked stock and notify the QA inspector. Note the damage on the delivery note.

## 3 Visible product damage

If the product itself is visibly damaged, post the affected quantity to blocked stock, label the handling unit "BLOCKED - QA", and notify the QA inspector. Do not mix damaged and undamaged units on one pallet.

## 4 Broken seal or moisture

If a seal is broken or the packaging is wet, move the handling unit to the quarantine area Q-01, post it to blocked stock and notify the QA inspector. Do not open wet packaging.

## 5 Suspected contamination

If there is a leak, an unknown substance or an unusual odour, stop unloading immediately. Isolate the area, keep other people away and do not touch the material. Notify the QA lead and EHS at once. Unloading resumes only after EHS has cleared the area.

## 6 Photos

Photos must show the handling unit label and the damage. Attach them to the goods receipt in the WMS.

## 7 Responsibilities

The QA inspector decides whether blocked material is returned, reworked or released.
