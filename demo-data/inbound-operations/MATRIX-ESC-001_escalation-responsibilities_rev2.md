# MATRIX-ESC-001 Inbound Escalation and Responsibilities Matrix (Revision 2)

> Synthetic demonstration document. Kestrova Components GmbH is a fictional company. Not for operational use.

## 1 Purpose

This matrix names who decides what during inbound receiving at HAM-01 and how fast they respond.

## 2 Matrix

| Topic | Responsible role | Response time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Quantity deviations above tolerance, recounts | Shift lead | 30 minutes |
| Missing, wrong or unreadable labels and batch numbers | Shift lead | 30 minutes |
| Damaged material, quality holds, missing certificates | QA inspector | 2 hours |
| Suspected contamination, leaks, unknown substances | QA lead together with EHS | Immediately |
| WMS errors not solved by GUIDE-WMS-003, unknown error codes | WMS key user | 1 hour |
| Over-deliveries, closed purchase order lines | Purchasing | Same working day |
| Conflicting or unclear instructions between documents | Process owner (inbound) | Same working day |

## 3 Rules

- Escalate to the responsible role in the matrix; the shift lead is the fallback contact during the shift.
- When two approved documents give different instructions, stop and escalate to the process owner. Do not choose one instruction yourself.
- Safety comes first: suspected contamination is escalated immediately, even outside working hours.
