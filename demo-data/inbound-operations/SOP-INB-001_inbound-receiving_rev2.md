# SOP-INB-001 Inbound Receiving Procedure (Revision 2)

> Synthetic demonstration document. Kestrova Components GmbH is a fictional company. Not for operational use.

## 1 Purpose

This procedure describes how inbound deliveries are received at the Hamburg distribution centre (site HAM-01).

## 2 Scope

All inbound deliveries of purchased material at HAM-01.

## 3 Responsibilities

- The warehouse operator unloads, checks and counts the delivery and posts the goods receipt.
- The shift lead confirms deviations and decides on blocked deliveries.

## 4 Procedure

### 4.1 Arrival and document check

Check that a delivery note is present and refers to an open purchase order.

### 4.2 Quantity check and tolerance

Count every delivery line. Deviations of up to 5% of the ordered quantity are posted as counted. Larger deviations are reported to the shift lead before posting.

### 4.3 Quality-managed material

Quality-managed material is posted to quality inspection stock. Exception: material from a certified supplier with a valid certificate on file may be posted directly to unrestricted stock (direct posting).

### 4.4 Batch-managed material

Record the batch number before posting.

### 4.5 Goods receipt posting

Post the goods receipt in the WMS against the purchase order line.

## 5 Condition check

Inspect every handling unit for damage. Report damage to the shift lead.
